# Manual Golden Flow Checklist

1. Open http://localhost:8081/examples/plain-html/ in Incognito.
2. Open http://localhost:3000/.
3. Start a visitor conversation.
4. Send a unique test message.
5. Confirm it appears in the dashboard.
6. Reply from the dashboard.
7. Confirm the visitor receives the reply.
8. Refresh the visitor page.
9. Confirm identity/history behavior.
10. Disconnect and reconnect.
11. Confirm messages are not duplicated.
